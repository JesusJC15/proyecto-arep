# Chest Pain Escalation Signals

Chest pain, chest discomfort, or pressure associated with fatigue, dizziness, or shortness of breath should be handled as a higher risk pattern in a triage setting. Even without diagnosis, combined cardiorespiratory symptoms increase the need for professional assessment.

Escalation is more defensible when symptoms worsen with light effort, recur during the same day, or appear together with a history of hypertension or other chronic cardiovascular concerns. The recommendation should clearly say that the collected evidence supports review by a professional rather than simple observation.

For demo purposes, this document represents the class of signals that justify professional review because the risk comes from the combination of symptoms and context, not from a single isolated word.
