# Hydration and Rest Guidance

Hydration, sleep, and reduction of intense activity are reasonable support measures for mild discomfort, transient cough, or general tiredness without red flags. The patient should be encouraged to monitor change over time instead of assuming that all symptoms are harmless.

Support advice becomes secondary when symptoms intensify, breathing worsens, chest pain appears, or the patient cannot continue normal activity. The value of this guidance is highest for low severity scenarios that still need explicit safety boundaries.

In a triage demo, hydration and rest content helps the recommendation explain what to do now while preserving the rule that any deterioration should trigger a new evaluation.
