# Respiratory Watchful Waiting

Persistent cough, throat discomfort, moderate fever, nasal congestion, and tiredness can remain in watchful waiting when breathing is preserved and no severe chest symptoms appear. These cases benefit from hydration, rest, and observation during the next twenty four to forty eight hours.

Watchful waiting becomes weaker when cough is accompanied by shortness of breath, chest pain, confusion, or rapid worsening. The platform should emphasize return precautions, especially if the person feels progressively worse or cannot maintain normal activity.

This category is useful for cases that are not clearly self care but do not yet show strong red flags. The recommendation should reinforce follow up and warning signs rather than false reassurance.
